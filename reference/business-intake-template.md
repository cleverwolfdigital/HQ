# Business Intake Template

**Type:** SOP
**Category:** Operations
**Source:** Portfolio management build (2026-07)
**Related:** [PORTFOLIO.md](../PORTFOLIO.md), [SYSTEM.md](../SYSTEM.md)

Standard interview to capture the real state of each business before it goes on the portfolio dashboard. Answers populate that business's `projects/` file and drive its tier + cadence.

---

## The 10 questions

1. **One line** — what is it / what does someone pay for?
2. **Stage** — idea · building · live pre-revenue · operating · scaling · winding down
3. **Money** — revenue $/mo · rough profit or margin · cash/assets/debt tied to it
4. **Ownership** — you 100%? partners & %? investors?
5. **What exists** — [ ] entity/LLC · [ ] business bank account · [ ] website · [ ] paying customers
6. **Customers** — who buys · roughly how many · how they find you
7. **90-day goal** — what winning looks like this quarter
8. **#1 bottleneck** — the single biggest thing in the way
9. **Your time** — hours/week now · too much / too little / right
10. **Daily 1–2 things** — recurring action that would move it (or "nothing until ___")
