# Clever Wolf — Ideal Customer Profile

**Type:** Reference
**Category:** Marketing
**Date:** 2026-06-25
**Related:** [[cleverwolf-brand-positioning]]

---

## Primary ICP

**Business owners who know AI is useful but have no idea what to do.**

They don't want to understand the tech. They want someone to show up, install it, and explain it in simple terms.

### Key traits
- Aware AI exists and matters — not skeptics
- Overwhelmed by the options and complexity
- Don't have time or desire to figure it out themselves
- Want outcomes, not education
- Will pay for someone to just handle it

### What they're NOT
- Tech founders or developers
- People who want to DIY
- People who need to be convinced AI is real

---

## Business type

**Boring brick-and-mortar businesses.**

Not startups. Not tech companies. Traditional businesses — retail, trades, local services, professional services — that have been operating the same way for years and are starting to feel the pressure.

### Their emotional state
- **Desperate** — they know AI is coming and they know they'll fall behind
- **Paralyzed** — they just can't install it themselves
- **Anxious at night** — *"My competitor is going to figure this out before I do"*
- They feel the urgency but don't know the first step

### The gap they represent
They know agents, automations, and apps exist. They've heard about the internet economy. But the tech world speaks a language nobody understands — and nobody has shown up to translate it into their world.

---

## How this shapes messaging

- **Never lead with tools** (no "we use GPT-4, Claude, LangChain...") — lead with the outcome
- **Simple language** — "we install it, train you, maintain it" not "we deploy agentic workflows"
- **Service-first framing** — "we show up" is the value prop, not the technology
- **Two clear choices** — enhance what you have OR hand it off entirely

---

## Website copy direction (cleverwolf.ai)

**Headline candidates:**
- "AI for your business. Handled."
- "You don't need to understand AI. You just need it working."
- "We install it. We train you. You grow."

**Subheadline:**
> Most business owners know AI can help them. Almost none know where to start. We show up, build the system, and explain it in plain English — so you get the results without the headache.
