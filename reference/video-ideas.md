## Icahn Method

**Concept:** Find a video that already did well (100k+ views) but came from a small channel (under 30k subs) and wasn't even that well made. Steal the IDEA — not the title, not the thumbnail — just the idea. Then make a better version.

**Why it works:** Proven demand, low execution bar to beat, small channel means the topic hasn't been maxed out yet.

**Action:** Search YouTube for outlier videos in your niche — high views, small channel, mediocre production. Pick the idea and outexecute it.

**How to start:** Open Claude, paste in the free Idea-Finder prompt (from the video description), and have it pull videos in your niche that went over 100K views from channels under 30K subs. Pick ONE proven idea. Don't copy the title — copy the concept, then make it better.
