# What is really the issue? (six-month look back)

**Type:** Personal reflection
**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Asked by Quincy:** "I thought vibe coding and figuring out AI would help me make more money and
understand my money problems, but everything is still the same. If it's not the tools, and we're
finally reaching out to potential customers, what is really the issue looking at everything over
the past 6 months?"

Built from Command Center (31 businesses, ~80 projects, 387 open tasks), the CWD Finance ledger,
Howl's send records, CWD-Sprint and the HQ vault. It only sees what's written down. Health,
family, the wedding and the travel aren't in it, and they count too.

---

## The short answer

The tools worked. They did what they were pointed at, and they were pointed at **building**,
not at **selling to people who already know you** or at **looking at the money**. AI made building
nearly free, so the building multiplied, and every new thing added owner tasks, servers and
monthly costs without adding a customer.

The July 1 intake already said it: *every focus business has infrastructure but zero customers,
and the bottleneck is Quincy himself, starting and consistency, not money or resources.* Three
months later that is still the best one-line diagnosis.

---

## 1. Building took the place of selling

- July 1 narrowed ~31 businesses to 5. Command Center still lists 31, and roughly 80 projects
  exist. September and October alone started a catering marketplace, Aliʻi Made, GameD, Growth
  Engine, Brain Hub, an email scheduler and the CFDE cohort.
- What got built and launched mostly has no buyer yet: Min Plastics (zero orders since the
  Aug 18 launch), Learning to Learn (no real purchase proven), CFDE (built, no enrollees),
  Game Grader (staging), Opala Hoa (still migrating).
- The question on the table each week turned into "what should we build next," when it needed
  to be "who did I ask for money this week."

## 2. The sales that were planned never happened. The automated version did.

- The warm reactivation plan (email the people who already know and trust you: Times,
  Territorial, Maryknoll, Lex Brodie's, Hawaii Self Storage, La Salle, LUNA) was declared
  "week one" on Aug 12 and again on Sep 8. **The warm send log is still a header row.**
- Cold automation went out instead: **893 website-offer emails, zero human replies.** Then the
  HanDI re-mail. Then Growth Engine, built because the goal became "stop doing sales and
  marketing entirely."
- Every dollar that actually came in during this stretch came from a relationship: CWD
  retainers, Lifeline ($1,832/mo), La Salle's $2,000 ad buy, the Spotify Hawaii proposals,
  HomeAid. None came from a tool.

**The thing that makes you money is you, talking to people who know you. That is the one part
that keeps getting automated away.**

## 3. The money couldn't be seen, so it couldn't be managed

- 11,937 of 12,531 imported transactions are uncategorized. The biggest "expense" in every
  entity is literally *Uncategorized*, about $32.6k a month, and $24.9k of that is Venmo,
  Cash App, Apple Cash and ATM.
- About $35.8k/month going out against CWD's ~$30k coming in, with ~$12k cash across 31
  accounts. (Balances were stale when that was measured, so treat it as a warning, not a
  verdict. The Monday pass will firm it up.)
- $255.8k moved by Square instant transfer this year, about $4.5k in fees. GM Financial
  bounced three times in September. Checks kick back. CWD's state annual reports are
  delinquent, so the company isn't in good standing.
- Money moves between personal, CWM, CWD, Spot, Kapwa, Shaka's and Bada Bing with no record of
  who owes whom.
- The "what does CWD need to make to be profitable" note has said **not started** since June 18.

The tools can't fix money problems they can't see. Building a finance app came before looking
at the statements, and the looking is only starting now (Oct 8).

## 4. Too many businesses quietly drain the one that pays

CWD is the cash engine and isn't "in focus." Meanwhile it funds Kapwa (COGS never once measured,
a personal guaranty on the lease, a Square loan taking 12.5% of card sales), Shaka's
(QuickBooks empty), the Bada Bing lease talks, the vending business, and the hosting and API
bills for every app above. Each one looks small. Together they are the gap.

## 5. Everything still ends at you, and nobody can see that list

Look at what blocks the projects: drag 11 PandaDoc signature fields, set staff passwords,
reconnect Stripe, add the Square webhook, file the annual report, top up API credit, approve
the cold sequences. Each takes minutes. There are 387 open tasks. AI didn't take work off your
plate, it moved the work to a long tail of small owner clicks, and that tail grows faster than
anyone can clear it.

---

## What would change things

None of these needs a new tool.

1. **One hour a day of selling to people who know you,** before any building. Calls, texts,
   personal emails. Log every one. Target: 10 conversations a week. Start with the CWD client
   list and the people in your phone.
2. **Pick 1 to 3 businesses on the money, not the excitement.** For each of the others, decide
   this month: close, sell, hand off or freeze. A frozen business gets no new code and no new
   spend.
3. **No new build until the last one has a paying customer.** Write it down as a rule for every
   agent (Claude and Codex) to enforce, and say no for you.
4. **Keep the Monday money pass** until every account ties out and you can say, out loud, what
   CWD needs to bring in each month to be profitable.
5. **Stop the money leaks we already know about:** switch Square to free next-day transfers,
   file the annual reports, stop moving money through Venmo/Cash App without a note.
6. **Clear owner clicks in one weekly block** (30 to 60 minutes) so they stop holding up whole
   projects.

---

## How to read this later

If six months from now things still feel the same, check three numbers before anything else:

- How many people did I personally ask to buy something this month?
- Do I know, to the dollar, what came in and what went out last month?
- How many businesses am I running, and which one pays for the others?
