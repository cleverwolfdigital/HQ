## The operating principle

You have ~31 entities. Touching all of them by hand every day is impossible and would make everything shallow. Instead:

- **Tier 1 (Revenue Engines)** — you (or the operator) work these **daily**.
- **Tier 2 (Growth Bets)** — one **focused block per business per week**, on a rotation.
- **Tier 3 (Media / Niche Sites)** — these get "1–2 things a day" from **pipelines, AI, or a VA — not your calendar**. Your job is to build the pipeline once (via CleverWolf.ai) and review output, not to produce daily.
- **Tier 4 (Pipeline / Investments)** — **monthly** review or diligence checkpoint.

> **The unlock:** Tier 3 is exactly the work CleverWolf.ai exists to automate. Treat your own portfolio as CleverWolf.ai's first client. See the [Automation backlog](#automation-backlog).
