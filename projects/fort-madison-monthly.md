The other focus businesses are blocked by *Quincy's* starting-hesitation — solvable by him just doing it. **This one is blocked by geography.** Local news needs local presence; it structurally *cannot* be a solo-from-Hawaii operation. That's not a weakness to fix — it's the operating model: **this is the one business in the focus 5 that must be delegated to a local team to work at all.**

The #9 worry ("will they understand the vision?") is the real lever, and it's solvable: **you can't transfer a vision you haven't written down.** A 1-page editorial brief turns "hope they get it" into "here's the standard." Then hire one local stringer/correspondent, not staff-plural.
