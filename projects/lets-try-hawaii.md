## Two reframes (flagged 2026-07-01)
1. **The out-of-Hawaii audience is the market, not a problem.** People outside Hawaii reading a "try Hawaii" site are **trip-planners** — the highest-value travel audience there is (hotels, tours, activities, booking affiliates). Lean content toward "what to try / how to plan," not local-resident reviews.
2. **"Menus change so it may be done" is a blocker dressed as a fact.** A review/photo essay is experiential — "here's what we tried, here's the vibe." It doesn't expire when a menu updates. This is the same "getting out of my shell" hesitation from #8, rationalized. The cure is publishing one, not perfecting the archive.

**Needle-mover:** a dead-simple weekly publishing rhythm off the existing photo backlog. This is also a prime candidate for a **CleverWolf.ai content pipeline** — the automation that removes the "getting started" friction (draft-from-photos → your quick edit → publish).
