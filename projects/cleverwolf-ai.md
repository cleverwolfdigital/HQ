## The strategic tension (flagged 2026-07-01)
The **goal** (10 clients + 5×$10k sites from $0, nothing productized) and the **bottleneck** (trying to learn *all* AI while training others) point in opposite directions. An idea-stage service business is not moved by learning everything — it's moved by **productizing ONE offer and selling it**. This is the same root as the paused [launch](cleverwolfai-launch.md): *"Do not sell what you haven't internalized."* Narrowing the offer resolves both at once.

**Reframed daily 1–2:** not "learn all AI" → (a) advance the ONE productized offer, and (b) one sales/outreach action toward the first client.
