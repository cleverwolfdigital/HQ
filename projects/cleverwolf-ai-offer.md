## The core fix: menu → product
The current site (cleverwolfdigital.com/ai) sells *"AI added to all 6 services."* That's a **menu**, and a menu is unsellable by one person because it demands you master everything — which is exactly the bottleneck you named ("so much changes, I have to learn everything"). The fix is to sell **two productized things** you can deliver in your sleep, and let the full menu be the upsell later.

---

## Offer 1 — Entry: "AI Visibility Audit" (the wedge)
Convert the site's existing *"Free AI Analysis"* into a productized, paid deliverable.

- **What it is:** A report showing where the client's category is being searched inside ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google AI Overviews, where they're invisible, and a prioritized AI-opportunity map for their marketing.
- **Price:** $1,500–$2,500 (or free for the first 1–2 warm clients as proof, then paid).
- **Timeline:** 1 week.
- **Deliverable:** ~10-page report + a 30-min walkthrough call.
- **Why it wins:** Mostly AI-assisted to produce (low effort), demonstrates expertise, low-risk first "yes," and it **naturally recommends the website** as step two.

## Offer 2 — Flagship: "The $10k AI Website" (the goal)
This IS your stated 90-day product. Productize it hard: fixed scope, fixed price, fixed timeline.

- **What it is:** A conversion-focused website with AI built in — a lead-qualifying AI assistant, GEO/AEO content so they appear in AI answers, and analytics/conversion tooling. Your local homepage build is already the visual template.
- **Price:** $10,000 (optional $500–$1,500/mo care plan = recurring revenue later).
- **Timeline:** 3–4 weeks, productized stages (kickoff → build → AI integration → launch).
- **Deliverable:** Live site + AI assistant + a loom on how it works.

## 90-day plan
- **Weeks 1–2 — Productize & internalize:** Write the two 1-page offer sheets (scope/price/timeline/deliverable). Deliver ONE audit free to a friendly client as your template. *This is how you "internalize before selling" — by doing it once, not by learning all AI.*
- **Weeks 3–4 — Build the delivery kit:** A repeatable audit process (AI-assisted) + productized website production checklist from the existing homepage template.
- **Weeks 5–12 — Sell the book:** 1 warm-client touch/day. Book reviews → deliver audits → upsell websites. Track toward 10/5.
